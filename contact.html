Questions

Can we bring our existing certificates and registers?

Yes. We import your customers, units and due dates from Excel, so your registers carry on where they are.

Can we use our own certificate layout?

Your logo, company details, wording and signatures appear on every certificate. We set up your layouts with you.

Which standards are supported?

Checklists and standards are set per equipment type, so you work to the standards your clients require, such as SANS, ISO and EN standards.

Where is our data stored?

In a secure cloud database with daily backups. Each company's data is kept separate, and only your users can see it.
