How a certificate moves through LiftCertify

The same four steps for a single shackle or a full site visit.

  1. Inspect

    Pick the unit, mark each checklist item OK or defect, add photos and load test values.

  2. Approve

    The certificate waits as a draft until your LMI, or NDT Level 2 / 3, approves it.

  3. Send

    One click creates the PDF with your logo and signature and emails it to the client.

  4. Remind

    LiftCertify tracks every due date and tells you, and your client, what is coming up.
