Inspect on site, even without signal In development

Inspectors work on a phone or tablet: pick the unit, tap OK or defect on each item, take photos and capture load test values. Everything is stored on the device and synchronised when there is coverage again, so work underground or on remote sites is never lost.

  • One tap per checklist item, or a whole section at once
  • Photos and defect notes against the item they belong to
  • Inspector signs on screen

LMI approval and a full audit trail

Certificates stay drafts, without a signature, until someone qualified approves them. LiftCertify checks that their registration is still valid on the day.

  • LMIs approve lifting certificates, NDT Level 2 / 3 approve NDT reports
  • Changing an approved certificate removes the approval
  • Every creation, change, approval, print and email is logged with who and when
  • Roles per user: Admin, LMI, lifting tackle inspector, NDT technician, office, manager

NDT and magnetic particle inspection reports

A dedicated NDT division with MPI reports to ISO 9934-1: test parameters, every area tested with its result, photos with captions, and sign-off by the technician and a Level 2 / 3 approver.

  • Test areas per equipment type, such as a truck mounted crane's booms, lugs and stabilisers
  • Technique, current, medium, lighting, field check and acceptance criteria
  • Technician scheme, level and certificate number on every report
